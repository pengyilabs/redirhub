Best value
Why per-link pricing hurts you: Most link management platforms charge $0.05–$0.50 per additional link. As your redirect portfolio grows, costs spiral. RedirHub flips the model: you pay for domain capacity, and every plan includes unlimited redirect records. A team managing 5,000 links on Bitly would pay ~$320/mo on higher tiers — RedirHub Core handles it for $49/mo.
Volume capacity is intentionally secondary. Most customers can choose Core or Pro without opening these controls. Pro is the recommended choice for business-critical redirect infrastructure. Larger customers can expand a single dimension directly, while annual billing uses the same simple rule: pay for 10 months and receive 12.
