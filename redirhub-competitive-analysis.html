Pricing Strategy

How They Charge

Per-Link Cap

Pay more as you create more links. Monthly creation caps force tier upgrades.

Used by:

Bitly, Rebrandly, BL.INK

Problem: Steep jumps between tiers. Going from Growth to Premium on Bitly is an 8x price increase just because you need more links.

Per-Hostname

Pay by number of domains managed. Traffic volume usually unlimited.

Used by:

redirect.pizza, urllo, RedirHub

Advantage: Scales with infrastructure you actually use. No artificial link caps. RedirHub differentiator: unlimited redirect records within each tier.

Hybrid

Mix of link caps, click tracking limits, feature gates, and per-seat charges.

Used by:

Short.io, BL.INK (click caps)

Complex but flexible. Short.io is the most affordable hybrid option ($5/mo entry).

RedirHub Positioning

Where RedirHub Wins

Competitive Advantages

  • Unlimited redirect records — no per-link cap like Bitly/Rebrandly
  • Unlimited team members included on all plans (others charge per-seat)
  • 90ms latency — claims 4x faster than Bitly
  • 100M+ redirects/day — proven at scale
  • 87% savings vs Bitly Premium ($300/yr vs $2,388/yr)
  • Free tier usable for real work — 100K visits/mo vs Bitly's 10 links
  • API + MCP server for automation

Market Opportunity

Bitly's biggest weakness: Per-link pricing model punishes growing teams. Every competitor is positioning against it.

Price-sensitive segment: Freelancers, startups, and agencies are actively looking for alternatives. Multiple comparison articles highlight 87% cost savings.

Technical edge: 90ms latency + wildcard domains + bulk CSV import gives RedirHub an infrastructure story that pure link shorteners can't match.

Gaps to watch: Brand recognition (Bitly is synonymous with "short link"), compliance certifications (BL.INK wins here), and integration ecosystem (Bitly has HubSpot, Salesforce, Zapier).
